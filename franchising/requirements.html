Franchise Requirement

What we need

Requirements Details
Floor area 180 to 300 m²
Frontage At least 8 m on the street
Money to invest At least US$90,000
Franchise fee US$25,000, then 5% of monthly sales
Owner on site Full time for the first year
Training 3 weeks in Phnom Penh

From signing to opening

  1. Sign the agreement.
  2. We plan your floor and buffet line.
  3. Build and fit out the shop.
  4. Your team trains with us.
  5. Open, about 16 weeks after signing.
