Franchise FAQs

The great place for your most asked questions!

How much does it cost in total

Between US$190,000 and 270,000, including the shop fit-out, equipment and the franchise fee.

Do I need restaurant experience?

It helps, but it is not required. Our training crew works with your team for your first weeks.

Can I open in a shopping mall?

Yes, as long as the mall allows a kitchen exhaust and has enough power for the induction burners.

Where do the broths come from?

Our central kitchen in Phnom Penh makes every broth base and delivers it each week.

Can I add my own dish?

You can add up to three local dishes to the buffet after we taste them. The broths stay the same.

If you have any more questions, feel free to contact us by clicking on Contact us
