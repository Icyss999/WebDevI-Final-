Our History

From one pot on a street corner to a table full of friends all over Phnom Penh.

HOW IT STARTED

Uncle Nha's first pot

Pu Nha started with a single charcoal stove, one pot split into two broths, and a recipe for bone and herb soup handed down from his mother. Neighbours came for the soup and stayed for the company. Two decades later, we still simmer that same broth for 12 hours every single morning.

Through the years
